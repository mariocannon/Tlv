Verity: tap a product to prove it's real

Each product carries a small NFC tag with its own secret key. Tap it with any phone and the page tells you in plain words whether it's genuine. No app needed.

Why a copy fails

  1. Every tap, the tag creates a fresh one-time code signed with its secret AES key.
  2. Our server checks the signature and that the tap counter has gone up.
  3. Reuse an old link, or copy the tag without the key, and the check fails on the first tap.

What this demo includes

CardForge (Android): staff encode real NTAG 424 DNA tags, then quality-check them.
Tap page: what a shopper sees after tapping.
Staff dashboard: live scans, alerts and tags.

Verity is a working name. This is an MVP for testing and demos.
