Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Verity checks whether a product is genuine when someone taps its tag. We keep what we record to the minimum needed to do that and to spot copied tags. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

When you tap a tag

We record:

  • the time of the check and its result (genuine, copied link, and so on)
  • which tag was checked, and the tag's code
  • a rough location: the city name from your phone's time zone setting, such as "Auckland"
  • your browser and device type, as your browser reports it

We don't ask for your name, email or an account, we don't use GPS or your exact location, we don't keep your IP address in our scan records, and we don't use cookies or tracking on the check page. Your IP address is used briefly to stop people flooding the checking service. Our hosting providers may hold it for a short time in their standard server logs.

Why we record it

  • To tell you whether the product is genuine. A code that has already been used means a copy.
  • To show the brand how often and roughly where their products are checked, so they can spot fakes and grey-market stock.

The brand that made the product can see the scans of its own tags. Brands can't see anything that identifies you, because we don't collect it.

When you apply for the pilot

The form on our home page collects your name, work email, company, and anything you choose to tell us about your product. We use it only to contact you about the pilot, and we don't share it or add you to a mailing list.

Brand accounts

If you use the Verity dashboard, we store your email address and sign-in details so you can log in. The dashboard keeps you signed in using your browser's local storage.

Where it's stored

Scan records, pilot applications and accounts are stored with our database provider, Supabase, in their Sydney, Australia data centre. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers may record standard web request logs. Both providers protect data under their own security and privacy terms.

How long we keep it

Scan records are kept while the brand uses Verity, because a tag's history is how copies are detected. Pilot applications we don't go ahead with are deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Until we have a dedicated address, use the form on our home page and say it's a privacy request. If you're not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
